Eye Hand Synth

An instrument you play with your face

Blink once with a hand-held steady face and the page also keeps two small eye photos, one open and one closed. Press Develop to register them and compare bicubic upscaling with a super-resolution network. Your video never leaves this page. Face and hand tracking run locally in your browser, nothing is recorded or uploaded. Use headphones, and keep your face and hand well lit. Mouse mode: move to look, click to blink, space for snare, keys 1 to 5 for fingers, arrow up and down for the filter.
